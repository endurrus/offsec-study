the one rule that saves runs

Prove control before perfecting the payload. Drop \xcc\xcc\xcc\xcc (int3) as the shellcode. If WinDbg breaks exactly where ESP pointed, your offset + jump are correct and any remaining failure is in the shellcode (badchars/encoding). This split saves hours.
